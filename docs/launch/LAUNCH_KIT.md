## One line

## Short description

Explore nearby retail variety, everyday amenities and transit with a simple score out of 10. No account required. Missing observations stay unknown. SiteBuddy describes map context, not business success.

## Medium description

Thinking about a location? SiteBuddy Free starts with three questions: what kinds of shops are mapped nearby, which everyday amenities appear, and how close is a mapped transit stop? Enter a public U.S. address, confirm the match, and see an equal-weight score with explanations and clear limitations. Share an address-free summary or try another location. It does not estimate demand, footfall, revenue, brand fit or lease suitability. Optional feedback helps us learn which deeper questions would be worth exploring next.

## Longer explanation

A location decision has many layers. SiteBuddy Free deliberately starts with only the public mapped surroundings. It reads openly licensed map context in a 600-meter straight-line radius and applies the existing transparent three-dimension rubric. It shows where observations are missing and withholds the total when any required dimension is unavailable. The model is illustrative and uncalibrated; a well-mapped transit-served area can score highly without being suitable for a particular business.

The app asks permission before sending an address to its public geocoder and confirms the location before retrieval. Shared summaries omit addresses and coordinates and are clearly labeled sender-provided. Replay downloads contain the location and observations, so they should be handled separately. Optional anonymous action counts are off by default. Feedback and future-interest choices are voluntary; email requires separate contact consent. Professional capabilities listed in the form are future ideas, not available products.

## Available Free capability statement

Address confirmation; live public map context; retail variety, everyday amenities and transit proximity; equal-weight Universal Site Score; explicit unknowns and score withholding; provenance; local replay snapshot; address-free sharing; voluntary feedback and future-interest capture.

## Limits statement

U.S. public addresses only. Data coverage and feature freshness vary. Straight-line proximity is not walking access or service quality. The score does not measure demand, demographics, footfall, competition, finances, opening hours or operating performance. It is not a lease recommendation, revenue forecast or brand-fit assessment. Providers may be unavailable. There is no paid intelligence or private enterprise engine in this Free app.

## FAQ

**Is it free?** Yes. No account or payment is needed for the current Free experience.

**What does a high score mean?** More of the rubric's mapped variety, amenities and nearby transit are present. It does not establish commercial viability.

**Why no score?** A required dimension may lack usable observations, or a provider request failed. Unknown is not converted to zero or replaced with fabricated data.

**Does sharing reveal the address?** The default summary link does not include it. A replay download does include the address and coordinates. Do not confuse the two.

**Is a received summary verified?** No. It is sender-provided, editable and is not an independently validated assessment.

**Are you tracking my searches?** The app does not persist an address history. Providers receive the address/area needed for a consented lookup. Optional analytics collects fixed counts, never complete addresses or emails.

**Can I get demographics or a financial report?** Those are discovery categories only. No availability date or price is promised.

**Is the source open source?** The repository is publicly inspectable, but no general software reuse license has been granted. Third-party software and data retain their own licenses.
