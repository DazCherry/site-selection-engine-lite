# SiteBuddy distribution experiment

Research date: 7 October 2026. Recommendations are hypotheses, not observed market demand. No external posts or outreach have been sent.

## Positioning

SiteBuddy Free is a transparent first look at mapped retail variety, everyday amenities and transit near a public U.S. address. It is useful for starting a location conversation, not deciding a lease. It has no demographic, footfall, competition, revenue or brand-fit intelligence. Keep that boundary in every introduction.

## Patterns worth learning from

[Walk Score's methodology](https://www.walkscore.com/methodology.shtml) makes its named dimensions and limitations visible. SiteBuddy should likewise explain its score rather than imply a universal business-quality verdict. This is a presentation lesson, not permission to copy a formula or claim walkability equivalence.

[Placer.ai Anchor](https://www.placer.ai/anchor/about-the-anchor) offers a free entry into richer location insights. The useful product pattern is a clear initial benefit and a path to the next question. SiteBuddy has no real foot-traffic data and must not market itself as a replacement. Interest selections are discovery evidence, not a promise to build an equivalent product.

[GitHub's README guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/about-readmes) supports putting purpose and getting started where visitors can find them. Lead with a working demo, image and limits; keep reproducibility and source attribution directly accessible. Source-visible is the correct description: this repository does not grant a general open-source software license.

[Show HN's rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) favor something people can try and discuss, with a low barrier. SiteBuddy's no-account Free experience fits that format better than a waitlist page. The owner should explain the technical choices and ask for critical feedback, not votes. A new branding/distribution layer alone may not justify repeated Show HN launches; check prior submission history before posting.

[Product Hunt's launch guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) emphasizes makers and early adopters. Its [preparation guidance](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch) supports preparing visuals and a clear first comment, without a promised winning launch time. Broad maker traffic can test comprehension and sharing, but cannot establish professional willingness to pay. Do not solicit upvotes.

## Recommended sequence

| Stage | Audience and action | Learning objective | Proceed when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Quiet test | Owner invites 5-10 known testers, including a few operators, to use a public civic address | Can a new visitor understand the result and complete the path without explanation? | Material bugs fixed; users can explain what the score does and does not measure |
| 2. Builder feedback | GitHub showcase and one relevant maker conversation; Show HN only if appropriate under current rules/history | Reliability, clarity, share behavior, reproducibility | Failures are understood; core journey is stable under actual traffic |
| 3. Professional discovery | Small, individually relevant operator/broker/consultant conversations and permission-based community post | Which missing question affects an actual decision? | Repeated concrete needs appear across independent conversations |
| 4. Broader launch | Product Hunt or another broad community with prepared screenshots and active owner follow-up | Acquisition-to-use and sharing by source | Owner can respond; no known reliability/privacy defect; sufficient capacity |
| 5. Focused follow-up | Owner contacts only appropriate, consented interested people | Report vs subscription vs more discovery hypotheses | Owner chooses the next commercial experiment based on evidence |

Suggested numbers are planning targets, not statistical significance thresholds. Re-check community-specific promotion rules immediately before posting; no universal permission to post in broker/operator groups is assumed. Do not purchase ads or send bulk messages in this phase.

## Measurement and interpretation

Use only fixed utm_source values: github, hackernews, producthunt, community, outreach. Never append an address, email, person ID or private campaign label. The default shared link strips query data and records only shared as a coarse channel when consented. This intentionally loses detailed attribution for privacy.

Read opted-in page visits, analysis starts, address submissions/confirmations, successes, withheld results, failures, repeat actions, share attempts/copies, deeper CTA, feedback, interest submissions and category preferences. Separate public and synthetic events; exclude qa. Page visits are not unique people. Different consent rates and dropped requests mean cross-channel rates are directional. Direct can include unattributed traffic. Do not add incompatible counts into a fake conversion percentage.

For each small batch, record date, channel, voluntary visit count, public analysis outcomes, technical failures, sharing, qualified-interest count, top requested categories and a short owner-authored summary of conversations without private addresses. Use raw counts alongside ratios. The app does not link a person's contact record to their search or analytics history.

## Decision support, not automatic investment

- Many visits but few starts: test positioning/intake friction before expanding features.
- Many starts but few scores: prioritize provider reliability and explicit unknown handling.
- Successful analyses with little repeat/sharing: investigate whether the Free result answers a meaningful question.
- Repeated demand for one dimension among real-decision respondents: owner may investigate that Paid hypothesis.
- Requests for one specific deliverable: consider testing a one-time professional report before assuming recurring demand.
- Repeated workflow use with ongoing needs: investigate a subscription hypothesis through discovery.
- Brand-specific or confidential requests: reserve for a separate Private Engine discussion; do not move that data or method into this public project.

None of these observations automatically authorizes Paid development. A checkbox is weaker evidence than an independently described decision problem. Do not use votes, stars, traffic spikes or raw email count as proof of product-market fit.
